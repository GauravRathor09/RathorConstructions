— FAQ

COMMON

QUESTIONS

A standard 1,000–1,500 sq.ft single-floor house takes approximately 6–9 months from foundation to handover. Multi-floor projects (G+1, G+2) typically range from 10–16 months. Timelines depend on plot conditions, approvals, and finish level chosen — we give you a firm written schedule before work begins.
We offer both. A per sq.ft rate contract covers all civil and finishing work at a fixed per-sq.ft rate with no hidden extras. A full turnkey contract covers everything end-to-end — design, structure, finishes, electrical, and plumbing — under a single quoted price. Both models include a detailed cost breakdown before you sign.
We procure all materials directly from trusted vendors to ensure quality and on-time supply. If you have specific brand preferences (e.g. a particular tile or sanitary brand), we accommodate that and adjust the quote accordingly. You always retain the right to inspect material samples before approval.
Payments are milestone-based — typically split across foundation completion, slab casting, brickwork, plastering, and final handover. No large upfront lump-sum. The exact schedule is written into the contract so you know exactly when each payment is due and what work it corresponds to.
Our primary base is Haldwani, Nainital. We actively work across Uttarakhand — including Nainital, Ramnagar, Rudrapur, Kashipur, Almora, and Dehradun. For projects further afield, please contact us and we will assess site accessibility before confirming.
All completed projects come with a 1-year structural defect warranty in writing. This covers any cracks, seepage, or structural settling issues that arise post-handover at no additional charge. Electrical and plumbing fitments carry manufacturer warranties which we hand over to you at the time of key delivery.
